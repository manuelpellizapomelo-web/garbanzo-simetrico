# Conversor Masivo – Batch Image & Audio Converter

If you find this tool useful, you can support its development on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/mitio98).


Convert, compress and resize entire folders of images and audio files in one go. No installation, no internet connection, no ffmpeg needed. Just pick a folder, choose your settings and hit Start.

## Images
- **Input:** PNG, JPG, BMP, WEBP, GIF, TIFF, ICO (+ AVIF when supported)
- **Output:** WEBP, JPG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, GIF, ICO and PDF (+ AVIF)
- Adjustable quality (JPG / WEBP / AVIF / PDF)
- Resize by percentage or by maximum side length
- Reduce colors for smaller PNG and GIF files
- Grayscale, black & white (with adjustable threshold) and sepia filters

## Audio
- **Input:** WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AIFF, AU
- **Output:** OGG, OPUS, MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF
- Adjustable quality for lossy formats (OGG / OPUS / MP3)
- Convert to mono, lower the sample rate, normalize volume

## Why you'll like it
- Handles whole folders, including subfolders
- Progress bar, cancel button and one-click "open output folder"
- Choose whether to overwrite existing files
- Works 100% offline: your files never leave your computer
- Single Windows .exe, nothing to install

Perfect for game developers, artists, web designers, podcasters and anyone who needs to shrink or convert hundreds of files fast.

## Good to know
- Windows only.
- The interface is currently in Spanish.
- For animated GIF/WEBP files, only the first frame is converted.
- Windows may show a SmartScreen/antivirus warning because the app is not code-signed. Click "More info → Run anyway".
